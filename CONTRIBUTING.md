# Contributing

Contributions are welcome. Please open an issue or pull request for a paper that
fits at least one of the repository's two core tracks:

1. **Multi-reference image generation** — generation conditioned on multiple
   visual references, subjects, concepts, identities, styles, or control signals.
2. **Agentic image generation** — an LLM/VLM agent plans, invokes tools,
   critiques, verifies, or iteratively refines image generation or editing.

## Inclusion checklist

- Link to a primary source: arXiv, official proceedings, or OpenReview.
- Use the exact paper title and publication status.
- Link only official project pages and author-maintained code repositories.
- Add one sentence explaining why the paper belongs in the selected track.
- Do not list a benchmark as a generation method, or a single-reference method
  as multi-reference, without labeling it clearly.
- Mark unreviewed work as `arXiv` or `under review`; do not guess a venue.

## Entry format

```markdown
| Year | Paper | Venue / status | Resources | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | [Exact title](paper-url) | arXiv | [Project](url) · [Code](url) | One-line relevance. |
```

Please keep entries newest-first within each category and avoid duplicates.
